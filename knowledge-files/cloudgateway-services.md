## DLT registration for SMS

TRAI rules in India make DLT (Distributed Ledger Technology) registration compulsory for business SMS. The business, its sender ID (header) and SMS templates must be registered on an operator DLT portal; unregistered SMS are not delivered. Our team guides you through DLT registration and follows TRAI guidelines, including DND filtering and opt-out for promotional SMS.

## Which channel should I choose?

- **Bulk WhatsApp / WhatsApp API:** best engagement, two-way chat, rich media — customer must opt in.
- **Bulk SMS:** reaches every phone, even without internet; best for OTP and alerts.
- **RCS:** WhatsApp-like rich messages inside the SMS app with verified brand — Android only.
- **Voice call:** for people who don't read messages, rural reach, regional languages.
- **Email:** cheapest per message; newsletters and detailed content.
Many businesses combine them: WhatsApp for marketing, SMS for OTP and alerts, RCS for rich promotions.
